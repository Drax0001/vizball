# Surface brief: public site (home first)

Scope: all public pages (home, le-sport, tutoriels, actualites, boutique, gouvernance, media, forum, contact, auth, 404). Mode: Persuade on home; Read on le-sport, articles, gouvernance; Operate on boutique cart, forum, forms. Admin dashboard out of scope.

Audience: players in Cameroon, global curious/press, partners, existing community. Action: understand the sport, then "Découvrir le sport" / contact / join. Proof: the real rules, field, founding facts and photos only. No invented stats, testimonials or fixtures.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the pitch. Vizball's official 48 × 24 m field (great circle, peripheral circles, Z3/Z2/Z1, butoir) is the site's architecture and motif, drawn in chalk and animated with the real play loop. Refuses the federation default: photo carousel, stat bar, equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep pitch green ground (#143D2B and a darker night shade), chalk cream lines and type, gold for scoring and emphasis, brand red for the one hot action. Fester Heavy uppercase display, Fester Bold Italic for voice, Fester Regular body. Hairline chalk rules, pill controls, 14px media radii, circles and straight lines only.

STORY: In seconds the visitor sees a new sport's field and name, grasps 4 vs 5 and the two zones by watching the play loop, believes it is real and organised, and acts: discover the sport, watch tutorials, join or contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen dark pitch. The field draws itself in chalk across the viewport; the great circle holds a photo of the Target. Giant VIZBALL wordmark (BALL in gold) set bottom-left across the shooting zone; one-line subtitle under it; red pill "Découvrir le sport" bottom-right on the touchline; a small "Prochain match" widget top-right fed by real events (falls back to "Depuis 2007 · Cameroun"). Minimal top bar: logo, four key links, language, Menu.
